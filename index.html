One long, low wedge

Just under five metres long and barely 1.16 metres tall. The body is a single sharp line from the nose to the tail, with the cabin pushed forward and the engine laid out behind it.

A Y in the dark

The headlights draw a Y-shaped signature. The same shape returns in the tail lights, the air intakes and the hexagons across the engine cover.

Doors that open to the sky

Scissor doors hinge at the front and swing straight up. It's a trick that flagship V12 Lamborghinis have used since the Countach.

Built around the driver

Three screens: a cluster behind the wheel, a central touchscreen and a display just for the passenger. The drive-mode controls sit on the steering wheel, so your hands never leave it.

Twelve cylinders, three motors

A naturally aspirated 6.5-litre V12 makes 825 CV at 9,250 rpm. Three electric motors add to it, two on the front axle and one on the eight-speed dual-clutch gearbox behind the engine.
